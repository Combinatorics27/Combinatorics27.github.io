the [lump of labor fallacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lump_of_labour_fallacy) refers to the false belief that there is a fixed amount of work in the economy that needs to be done. or in other words that there is an exhuastive list of jobs humans can do.

technology has progressed exponentially since the industrial revolution (i maybe use the word exponential too liberally) but it hasn't led to large scale unemployment. majority of the jobs that exist today did not exist <1920.
