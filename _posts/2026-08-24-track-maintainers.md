source - [blood on the tracks](https://caravanmagazine.in/magazine/2025/10) 

more than 2.2 lakh track maintainers are employed by the indian railways. around 100 die each year, run over by incoming trains, usually due to parallel trains where the person moves from one line but is caught on the parallel line by another train.

other than those employed by the indian railways, there are contract workers as well. the official decade old number was around 90k, but an estimated 3 lakh contract workers work currently in track maintainance.

track maintainance is mostly a manual work. the indian railways employed are paid less than 30k per month. 

the temperature in the 'lines' is 4-5 degrees higher than the surroundings.
