eroom is moore spelled backwards. moore's law states that computing power grows exponentially with time. [eroom's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eroom%27s_law) states that the money required per approved drug increases exponentially with time. 

couple of the reasons mentioned in the paper that introduced the idea are - 

1. the better than beatles problem - we have drugs now for a wide range of diseases. and because of patent time-limits, we have mass generic versions of them. in order for a new drug to be approved, it needs to be significantly better than existing drugs. the first drug had to compete against placebo. but new drugs now have to compete with existing drugs.
2. cautious regulator problem - one of the issue with the amygdala is that it is afraid of quick negative things vs slow decay negative things. for example, cancer or road accidents are not given much importance whereas terror attacks are. but in terms of quantum of suffering, the former causes outshadow the latter. this is one of the reasons drug approving regulators now have stricter rules compared to the past. my opinion (formed from a single YT video) is that a lenient regulator can help increase the speed of medical innovation.
