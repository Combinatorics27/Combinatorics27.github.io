most mammals are dichromat. which means that instead of the three cone cells (red, blue and green) like humans have, they only have two. tigers look orange to us. but to deers, they look green. search for 'how does a tiger look to a deer'. 

each of ours' brain does this mistake of assuming that everyone sees the world just like we do. both literally and metaphorically. we often think other people are stupid. but what we don't realise is that how they see a situation might be completely different from us.

one person's brain might not even care to notice a dog on the street. but some other person's brain will react completely differently - directing the secretion of adrenaline. the same situation. two completely different reactions. 

so because each of our brains has developed differently, even the exact same situation can be perceived wildly differently by different people.
