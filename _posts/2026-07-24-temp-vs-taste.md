i rarely eat 'out of the kitchen' hot food. i recently did and noticed that while it did feel good, the taste was dulled. a ~~google search~~ llm prompt taught me that the relation between taste and temperature is inverted-u shaped. too cold and you don't get the aroma. too hot and the temperature dulls the taste receptors. there is a sweet spot where you get aroma + taste. 

thermic effect of food refers to the energy spent by the body in the process of digestion, synthesis and storage after you eat something. so for example if you eat 100kcal of food that is majorly protein, your body will spend 25kcal in digestion. the thermic effect is lowest for fat (0-5%), intermediate for carbs (5-10%) and highest for protein (20-25%).
