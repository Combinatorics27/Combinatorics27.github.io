one of the most painful conditions known to humans is cluster headaches. these are unique in that they occur in clusters of a dozen weeks at around the same time of the day and then the next cluster is around a year later. 

they are also called suicide headaches because the pain is so intense that before pain killers and other treatments, people would commit suicides to escape the pain. women who have delivered and also had the headache say the headache is far worse. 

[secret handshakes](https://gametheory.life/2018/07/22/inspired-by-goffman-the-secret-handshake/comment-page-1/#respond) are ways for people who do not recognise each other by face to meet at a pre-decided location. strangers usually avoid prolonged eye contacts and two people who intend to meet can decide beforehand that they will meet through the secret handshake of a prolonged eye contact.
