cisplatin is a chemical molecule. there's a platinum atom in the middle, surrounded by 2 chlorine atoms and 2 ammonia molecules. its cis because the chlorines and ammonias are adjacent. there's a transplatin where the chlorine sits opposite each other.

cisplatin is a chemotherapy drug. it enters the cells without needing to go through the cell membrane locks/receptors. it enters the nucleus and distorts the DNA which then has to be solved by cell death. 

remission rate is the proportion of patients whose symptoms go away. it does not mean cure in the context of cancer. cure is when there is no cancer growth after treatment for 5 or so years. remission measures the short term cancer effectiveness.

for testicular cancer, before cisplatin, the survival rate was 5%. 95% of people with this cancer died. after cisplatin, the numbers have reversed.

i remember reading in some feynman book about how his wife had some disease whose drug cure had already been discovered by the FDA or something. what is scary then is people who have lost their lives just before a drug was discovered or approved for use. the cure existed at the time of their death.
