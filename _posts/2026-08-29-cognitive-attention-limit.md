when i study, i study with 100% attention. no mobile phone nearby, no distracting object in field of view, etc. i've been noticing that when i study many new, difficult concepts in a day, my brain gets tired to the point that even reading something becomes difficult. i feel sleepy.

turns out recent [study](https://www.cell.com/action/showPdf?pii=S0960-9822%2822%2901111-3) has shown that after long periods of focused attention, the neurotransmitter glutamate accumulates in the prefrontal cortex, and excess is toxic so the brain then makes attention difficult and diverts you to lower cognitively demanding tasks. 

studying new topics is resource-intensive because it requires the prefrontal cortex. my personal limit seems to be 4 hours per day for new topics and 6-7 hours for revision.

also the brain requires lots of ATP for the intensive learning session which releases adenosine which signals the brain to sleep. the brain clears the adenosine during sleep. 

but the interesting thing is that this high energy is required only when learning something new. once you start practicing something, other parts of the brain take over and less of your brain resources are needed. so learning to drive initially will be cognitively taxing but once you're an expert, driving takes up less resources and isn't cognitively taxing as much as it is for a learner.
