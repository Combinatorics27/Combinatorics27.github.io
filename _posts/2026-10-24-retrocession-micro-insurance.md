an insurance company is what most of us are aware with - a company that agrees to share your risk in return for premiums. but the insurance company has its own insurers - called reinsurers. reinsurance companies take on the risk of insurance companies in return of premiums.

reinsurance companies exist because insurance companies are people-heavy and specialize in selling insurance products to a particular geography. reinsurers usually insure insurance companies from all over the world - thereby diversifying risk.

this structure makes the customer experience better. technically the customer's risk is covered by the insurer and multiple reinsurers, but it is impractical for the customer to go around to each of the companies asking for their claim, which is why reinsurers in the backend make sense. 

retrocession is when a reinsurer shares their risk with another reinsurer. so its insurance for reinsurer. 

micro-insurance refers to products that cater to low-income groups. low income groups are the most vulnerable to risk. but products need to be built differently for them since they don't have periodic cash flows like the salaried.
