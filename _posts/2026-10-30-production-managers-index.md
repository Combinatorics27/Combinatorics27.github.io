production managers index is a leading indicator to gauge the economy. its frequency is monthly. a representative sample of purchase managers is surveyed each month. purchase managers are those that are responsible for procurement and supply chain management in a company. so these are ideally placed in order to gauge the company's performance.

the questions ask about KPIs and have three options - improved, same, decreased. these are then weighted for different KPIs.

if everyone reports everything is same, PMI = 50. if everyone reports everything was same, we get a PMI of 50. if everyone reports everything increased, we get PMI of 100 and conversly 0.

usually PMI is within 45 to 60 for india. anything above 55 is good. 

OTIF stands for on-time, in full. this is a supply chain KPI. it measures the proportion of orders that were received on or before time, and in agreed quantity. so not less or damaged goods.
