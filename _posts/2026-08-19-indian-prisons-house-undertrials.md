title: "TIL: indian prisoners are majorly undertrials"
date: 2026-08-19
categories: til
---

(https://www.dataforindia.com/prisons/?utm_source=newsletter)[Data for India's] work on prison data shows that more than 3 out of 4 people in Indian prisons are undertrials, which means they haven't been convicted of an offence by the court yet and are undergoing the judicial process. Which means that many innocents are prisoners because the indian judicial system is slow.
