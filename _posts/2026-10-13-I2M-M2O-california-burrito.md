I2M stands Impressions to Menu and is a metric used for the resturants/cloud kitchens listed on delivery or other aggregator apps. it measures the percentage of people that see the name and preview of the restaurant and open its menu.

M2O stands for menu to order and is the successor of I2M in the funnel. it measures percent of people that order after opening the menu.

california burrito is an indian brand. an american, bert mueller came to india in 2010 for a study abroad program. and then started the first store in bangalore. latest financials report a revenue of 332cr and over 100 stores. 

i'm biased against india because i know the statistics so i'd like to pinpoint this excerpt from the 2012 wall street journal [article](https://www.wsj.com/articles/BL-IRTB-16813) - 

>Compared to the U.S., "things take much longer here," said Dharam Khalsa, the third business partner.  For instance, while it can take as little as an hour to open a bank account in the U.S., it has taken them more than 40 days to do so in India, he said. It took them four months  to register their company here, he added.


COCO stands for company owned, company operated model. california burrito owns all its stores and does not have a franchisee model.
