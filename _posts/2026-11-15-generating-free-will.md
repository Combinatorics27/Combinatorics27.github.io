having read a decent amount of robert sapolsky's words, i believe there is no free will. 

thanks to meta-awareness i've noticed myself acting a lot of times basis what evolution designed me to but which is not aligned with modern life. trivial example being eating sugar. 

one way then i think to generate free will (if there is something like that) is to construct your own rules. rules according to which you will take future decisions. so you're not a slave to the rules and incentives evolution designed for the humans that were hunter-gatherers. basically a way to combat evolutionary mismatch in some sense.

another way is to take decisions probabilistically. obviously there is some bias as to what the decision space is and how you decide it, but after obtaining the decision space, you use a random or a pseudorandom process to choose one of those decisions probablisitcally. 

stupid ideas i guess but i had to write it anyways since i don't have free will.
