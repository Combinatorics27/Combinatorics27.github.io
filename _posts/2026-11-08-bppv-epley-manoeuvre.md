[bppv](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/vertigo/symptoms-causes/syc-20370055) is a form of vertigo. vertigo is the phenomenon of your brain thinking its moving or swinging when infact it is completely still. 

this is caused due to calcium carbonate crytals in the inner ear being dislocated. their movement is used by the brain to determine the position of the body. 

the current best known way to treat it is called epley manoeuvre. it is a 3-4 minute positional, sequential exercise that aims to get the calcium carbonate crystals back to their original position. 

also because the inner fluid is low on calcium, another way it could heal on its own is through the calcium carbonate crystals dissolving over time. this can take over weeks. 

no medicine can cure this. medicines that are given only affect the brainstem and what they do is to make the brain less reactive to this signal from the inner ear, they do nothing to cure the actual cause.
