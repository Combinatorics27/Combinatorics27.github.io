flights are a great environment for talking to people, because atleast hitherto there isn't wifi on most planes. basically there are more constraints than a usual environment.

this is something that is trivial if you think about it but because of the inherent nature of the brain, it tends to forget it - that each of us knows very little. basically the 4 sets - known knowns, known unknowns, unknown knowns (does this even exist?) and unkonwn unknowns.

the last set is the largest in terms of cardinality. and you only come to realise it, which means shifting an element from the unknown unknowns set to the known unknown or known known set, when you talk with a new person or see something new. which probably is why a lot of people recommend travelling.
