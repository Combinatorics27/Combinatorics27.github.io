flights are a great environment for talking to people, because atleast hitherto there isn't wifi on most planes. basically there are more constraints than a usual environment, so the opportunity cost for talking with a stranger decreases. but then again when we scroll instead of talking to the person beside, that is because of path of least resistance than strictly due to opportunity cost. or rather the correct term is perceived opportunity cost. no one knows the true opportunity costs, we estimate them basis our knowledge. but i'm digressing.

one thing that is trivial if you think about it but because of the inherent nature of the brain, we tend to forget it is that each of us knows very little. basically the 4 sets - known knowns, known unknowns, unknown knowns (does this even exist?) and unkonwn unknowns.

the last set is the largest in terms of cardinality. and you only come to realise it, which means shifting an element from the unknown unknowns set to the known unknown or known known set, when you talk with a new person or see something new. which probably is why a lot of people recommend travelling.

what i learnt from a recent conversation was that there are IIITs specialising in design and that there are people who work as jewellery designers and watch designers. that there is this huge cohort of people working in design and fashion that i've never talked to. but they exist here as much as i do. i live in a tiny little bubble of my own that i call my world. but that world is basically zero compared to the whole world.

all my opinions, all of my beliefs and all the people i know, exist in this tiny bubble. so naturally it is humbling when you meet someone who has a bubble that is completely disjoint from yours, but just as real. 

explore or exploit can be seen from this lens. do you try to increase the bubble or do you try to make best of what's inside your bubble. from what i've read before, i think people should focus on expanding their bubbles when young. some expansion naturally happens when you go to college or to a job. but i think most of us don't try to expand it actively. i think expanding it actively is something we should be doing.
