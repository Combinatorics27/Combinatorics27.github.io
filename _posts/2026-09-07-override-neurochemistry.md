i've noticed that my actions are a function of motivation which is a function of my neurochemistry that day. extreme example being when i'm ill. i want to do nothing. not even the things i would otherwise call 'passion'.

but the extreme example aside, i've noticed the volatility in my motivation across days. some days are nice. some are bad. but this makes me a slave to my neurochemistry.

i'm starting a daily to-do list that i'll write down every morning. i cannot let neurochemistry control me. has to be the other way around.
