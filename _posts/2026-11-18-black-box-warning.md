usa's food and drug administration (fda) has what's called a black box warning for some medications. this is the most severe warning issued on drugs that can cause hospitalizaiton, depressive symptoms, death and other serious complications.

motelukast and levocetrizine are a drug combination widely prescribed and used in india for allergies like runny nose, sneezing, etc. in 2020, FDA issued a black box warning for montelukast because it has neuropsychiatric side effects in some people. it can cause depression, anxiety and suicidal thoughts.
