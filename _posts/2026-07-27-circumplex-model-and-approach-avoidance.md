the circumplex model of emotions plots emotions on a 2d grid. one axis is the valence that goes from positive to negative. the other is activation that goes from low to high. activation measures your physiological activity.

so the different quadrants are anger, depressed, excited, calm. 

another way of looking at emotions is through the approach-avoidance lens. some emotions make you motivated and do stuff. emotions like happy, excited and anger. some emotions make you avoid stuff. emotions like fear and anxiety.

anger is one of the few negative emotions that increases motivation. when you're angry you're willing to do stuff. 

different ways to look at the recent dharmendra pradhan resignation. but one lens is that the violence on students during the march invoked anger in people all over the country. anger motivates. thats what it did. it made people come up who otherwise wouldn't have.
