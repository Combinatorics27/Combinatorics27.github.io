[the zone of proximal development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zone_of_proximal_development) refers to the topics/concepts for an individual that the individual can learn only through the support or guidance of a teacher or mentor. the set of concepts can be partitioned into 3 subsets - concept the student can learn on their own without needing anyone's help, concepts that the student can learn but only when guided or helped by a teacher or mentor, and concepts that the student cannot learn even with someone's help.

institutes should focus on the zone of proximal development - concepts that students cannot learn on their own, but can, when guided by a teacher. 

the issue is that the zone is different for different students. which is why institutes have to run at the speed of the least capable student or the average student. the students who learn fast do not receive much return on attention employed in the classes. 

AI should be able to solve this within the next 1-2 years. i think we already have the techincal capabilities but no concrete product or something. i'm trying to build an AI system that teaches me at the edge of my current knowledge compared to classes that have to run at a certain pace and could be teaching topics one already knows from before.
