immortal jellyfish - the jellyfish [Turritopsis dohrnii](https://www.bbcearth.com/news/the-jellyfish-that-never-dies) never dies. well technically it does. it can be eaten by predators or die through other means. but it doesn't die aging. the cells of the dying jellyfish undergo transdifferentiation - a process where a mature cell converts into another mature cell. it basically converts its cells to the cells of its childhood state. and then it can grow into an adult again. and again and again. till death except from aging.

dna is the densest information storage system known to mankind. it is capable of storing 200 million GB worth of data in a single gram. the reason it is so dense is because of its 3d structure. dna storage is also very durable. it remains accurate for upto hundreds of years if kept in adequate conditions. the issue is information writing and retrieval. because these are microscales, its very difficult to write down stuff in the dna and difficult to retrieve it back. i think this is what the exponential growth after AGI will be. because there are a lot of things cracking which will make life so much better, there will be a ripple effect once we get smart enough AI systems. obviously assuming that ai is aligned. watching two opposite sentiments videos in a day is such a dissonance. delta4 method - attributed to kunal shah, it states that for a new startup to replace existing process, the delta in user rating should be atleast 4 on a scale of 10. so for example, chatGPT should be rated atleast 4 units more than google search or stackoverflow for it to replace the previous technologies. makes sense. humans are resistant to change. they are willing to change only when the marginal utility is more than the friction of change. 

UBP - like unique selling proposition, this is another startup concept lens. it stands for unique brag-worthy proposition. it relies on the fact that humans are status-driven animals. humans like it when they find something cool before others. and they then use that fact to boast. this works even with YT channels or comedians or movies. people like to signal that they found something before it became mainstream cool. 

transhumanism - this is the idea that science should focus on making humans superhumans. so longetivity, superorgans, cognitive enhancement. basically that we should not be constrained by what evolution gave us. given the speed of ai progress, i think atleast some of it - which is a huge thing - should be possible within my lifetime.
